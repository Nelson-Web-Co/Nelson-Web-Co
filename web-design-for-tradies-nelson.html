How people pick a tradie

Most people check you out online before they call. Here's where tradies lose work without knowing it.

They Google you before they call

Even if they got your number off your van or from a mate, most people search your name first. If nothing comes up, you look less established.

No job photos, no proof

Anyone can say "quality workmanship." A gallery of real finished jobs is what actually convinces someone you can do theirs.

They're comparing you to two others

Most people get 2–3 quotes. If the other two have a site and you don't, you're the odd one out before price even comes into it.

On-site, not at a desk

You're checking messages between jobs, not answering emails at 9am. A site that makes it a one-tap call beats one that expects a form and a wait.
