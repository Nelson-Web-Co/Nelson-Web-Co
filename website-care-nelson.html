Website care and hosting

Keeping your website up to date, working properly and showing up on Google.

Building a website is one part of it. Keeping it up to date, working properly and showing up on Google is the other.

Whether I built your site or not, I can help. If you're on Wix, WordPress or Squarespace and just need things changed, I can do that. If you'd rather not think about your website at all, a care plan means I keep an eye on it for you.

Changes on any platform

Need something updated? I can make changes to sites on Wix, WordPress and Squarespace as well as the ones I build, from a phone number to a whole new page.

Moving your website or domain

Changing hosts, moving off Squarespace or pointing your domain somewhere new can easily break your email if it's done wrong. I'll move it over carefully and test everything after.

Hosting

Your site is hosted on fast, secure servers, and every version is saved so it can be put back quickly if anything goes wrong.

Care plans and monthly checks

On a care plan I look after your changes and check your whole site each month, including making sure your contact form is still sending enquiries. See the plans

Getting found on Google

Every site comes with the basics done properly: page titles, descriptions, image text and submitting it to Google so it gets picked up.

Google Business Profile

I'll set up or tidy your Google Business Profile so you show up on Google Maps and in local searches, with the right hours, photos and contact details.
