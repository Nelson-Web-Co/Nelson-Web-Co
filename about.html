About Nelson Web Co

A local website designer in Nelson. One person, start to finish.

Blake McKayFounder, Nelson Web Co

Who you'll be dealing with

I started Nelson Web Co because I got sick of seeing hardworking business owners let down by bad websites.

Here's what I believe: if you own a business, whatever it is, you've taken risks most people never will. You deserve a website that reflects that.

If it takes longer to turn my phone on than to find what I need on your site, your customers are already gone. That's the standard I build to.

I'm a Nelson local with a Level 5 Business and IT qualification, a year of hands-on web development, and one goal: to help your business get found by the people who need you most. There's no agency or account manager in between. You deal with me, and I care how your site turns out.

I'm also a plasterer, so I know what it's like to run a trade business and how customers choose who to call.

How a job works

Simple and clear from the start, so you always know where things are at.

  1. A chat

    We talk about your business, what you need the site to do and any ideas you have. Over the phone or in person.

  2. A fixed price

    I send you a Scope of Work with everything that's included and a fixed price, so you know the cost before I start.

  3. I build it

    You see the site taking shape and send me one combined list of any changes you want before it goes live.

  4. It goes live

    I put it live on your domain and test everything. After that I'm still here for hosting, changes and anything else you need.
